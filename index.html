How the AI works

A digital twin of the fulfilment network

1 · Ingest

Orders, warehouse picks, routes, rosters, stock positions and delivery scans share one clock.

2 · Replay

Each order is walked through assignment, pick queue, pack, carrier truck, line-haul and a daily route queue.

3 · Attribute

For every late order the AI tests which fix alone would have saved it, then ranks causes by recovered on-time points.

4 · Recommend

Levers are simulated on the same orders, so every gain is a like-for-like replay.

About the data. This demo runs on a simulated 12-week network of about 110,000 orders generated in your browser with a fixed seed. Plug the same pipeline into live order, WMS, TMS, rostering and carrier feeds to get the same diagnosis on your own operation. All figures on this page are model output, not measured results.
